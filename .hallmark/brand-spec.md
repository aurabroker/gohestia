# Brand spec: kalkulator ERGO Razem (Aura Expert sp. z o.o.)

Read from the project's files on 2026-11-19, never from memory.

- **Logo:** `public/ergo-hestia-logo.jpg` (655 x 360, red ERGO over grey HESTIA on white). Used as found; it sits on the light plate so its white ground does not show on the buff paper. Never recoloured, never redrawn.
- **Brand red:** `#E4002B`, hover `#C00025` (`app/globals.css` before the redesign: `--color-czerwien`, `--color-czerwien-h`; the redesign brief names the same two values). Carried as `--color-accent` oklch(57.94% 0.2343 23.93) and `--color-accent-hover` oklch(50.96% 0.2058 23.3), which round back to those hex values.
- **Who runs the site:** Aura Expert sp. z o.o., agent of ERGO Hestia. The name stays visible in the header and the footer on every page; the site never poses as the insurer.
- **Action words:** primary "Sprawdź składkę", final "Wyślij zapytanie do agenta". Four steps: Wiek, Zdrowie, Oferta, Kontakt.
- **Not brand, replaced by the redesign:** Fira Sans and Fira Sans Condensed, the sage sheet, the blue of the user's entries (all chosen by the earlier redesign, not by the brand).
